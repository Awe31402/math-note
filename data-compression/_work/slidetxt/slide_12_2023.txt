     Q1.1
     What are the optimal quantization levels (intervals and reconstruction points) for a scalar
     one-bit quantizer under MSE distortion?
     Ans: The optimal quantization levels are C = {0.25, 0.75} and the corresponding
     intervals are S1 = [0, 0.5] and S2 = (0.5, 1].
