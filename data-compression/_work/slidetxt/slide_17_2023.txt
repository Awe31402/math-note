  called Weber’s Law

  We are constantly sampling a visual scene to align it with our fovea,
  called Foveation

- modeling low-level human
   vision features e.g. SSIM,

   perceptual metrics e.g. LPIPS

   supervised human subjective
   data e.g. VMAF, DISTS
SSIM
structural similarity
modeling low-level human vision features
Uses 3 key features to compare two images:
luminance, contrast, structure
SSIM
structural similarity
modeling low-level human vision features
But apply them over local patches instead of globally
- spatial non-stationarity of image features
- spatial non-stationarity of image distortions
- foveation
How:
calculate locally and then take mean
uses gaussian kernel for smoothening to model
foveation!
